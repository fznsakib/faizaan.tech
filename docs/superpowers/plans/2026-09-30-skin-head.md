# Skin Head — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (superpowers:test-driven-development for the pure texture/geometry helpers). Project skills that apply: `3d-model`, `tune-animation`.

**Goal:** A new head component that looks like the owner all over: his real photo face and front hair from the capture, with the rest of the head completed as him (skin, ears, neck, dark curly hair). No copper anywhere. It becomes the site's default head, and the existing copper head stays in the codebase, selectable.

**The owner's request** (2026-09-30):
> "Why don't you just put my head in there rather than just the face? That way the copper bits are gone! (Keep the copper head component for now.)"

**Constraint the owner has been told about:** the capture (`/Users/faizaan/Downloads/26_09_2026.glb`) is single-view. It has the face, front hair, one ear and some side; it has nothing for the back, top-back or far side. Anything the camera didn't see has to be synthesized, plausibly and in his colours. A proper 360° capture later will replace the synthesis; see Task 4.

## What exists (on `fznsakib/v2-next`)
- **`yarn headmap`** (`scripts/headmap.ts` + `scripts/headmap/*`): crops the capture to the face, aligns it to the stock head (`scripts/assets/base-head.glb`), transfers the face shape, and bakes the face skin. Outputs: `src/assets/head.glb` (with `TEXCOORD_0` front-projected UVs and `_FACEWEIGHT`) and `src/assets/head-face.jpg` (face-only 1024², room masked out).
- **`src/components/Head/index.tsx`** (the copper head):
  - behaviour: beat nod (`src/choreography/nod.ts`), mouse follow or phone tilt (`useDeviceTilt`), and camera fit (`src/choreography/fit.ts` `fitCamera`);
  - look: chrome material plus `faceSkin.ts` blend, lighting via Environment/Lightformers, and a rim light.
- **Head fit constants** `HEAD_WIDTH` / `HEAD_HEIGHT` / `HEAD_CENTRE` in `fit.ts` were measured from screenshots of the copper head.

## Approach
1. **Geometry:**
   - Start from the transfer pipeline, but widen what's transferred so the front hair volume and the visible ear and cheek come across too; the copper head's face-only crop excluded the hairline. Keep the blend to the stock head smooth.
   - Behind the capture's coverage, keep the stock skull, then give it a hair volume: a gentle outward offset over the scalp region (crown, back, sides above the ears), shaped to meet the captured front hair so the silhouette reads as his curly hair, not a bald mannequin. It must stay watertight: no cracks, spikes or hollow backs.
2. **UVs and texture atlas:**
   - Give the whole head one seamless UV layout. For example: the front-projected face UVs inside the face region, plus a cylindrical projection elsewhere, packed into one atlas with padding. Or do a full cylindrical unwrap with the seam at the back of the head hidden in hair.
   - Bake a single colour atlas (≤ 2048², JPEG ≤ ~600 KB):
     - **Face / front hair / visible ear:** from the capture's photo, masked exactly as today, so no room pixels.
     - **Skin elsewhere** (neck, far ear, sides, the back of the neck): sampled from his face's skin near the jaw and cheeks, with low-frequency variation and fine noise so it isn't flat, and colour-matched across the boundary.
     - **Hair elsewhere:** synthesized from the capture's own hair region, e.g. tile or patch-match samples of his curly hair texture, or a procedural curly-hair texture tinted with the hair's measured colour, blended into the captured hairline.
   - Blend every boundary with feathered masks: no visible seam where photo meets synthesis.
3. **Material and lighting for skin:**
   - Non-metallic; roughness ≈ 0.5–0.65 for skin and higher for hair; no copper emissive.
   - The photo has baked lighting, so keep the scene light soft on the face.
   - The site's Environment/Lightformers were designed for chrome; the skin head may carry its own gentler lighting setup inside its component.
   - Keep the beat-driven accents that make sense on skin (e.g. the rim light pulsing with snares); drop the copper emissive.
4. **A full-capture path, for later.** `yarn headmap` should accept a 360° capture. When the capture's coverage is closed (detect it, or take a `--full` flag), crop shoulders and background, align, and use the capture's own geometry and texture directly, skipping synthesis. Write this path and test it on a synthetic closed head (e.g. a UV sphere with a texture). Document in CLAUDE.md how the owner should capture:
   - use a 360° scanning app (Polycam, Scaniverse, RealityScan, or a TrueDepth head-scan app);
   - sit on a swivel chair and turn slowly while the phone stays still, or have someone walk around you;
   - use even light and keep your hair still;
   - export GLB (or OBJ + texture).
5. **Components:**
   - New `src/components/SkinHead/` (the default in `App.tsx`).
   - Keep `src/components/Head/` (copper) unchanged in behaviour, selectable with `?head=copper`.
   - Move the shared rig (nod, sway, look from mouse/tilt, camera fit, squash) into a shared hook or module (e.g. `src/components/Head/useHeadRig.ts`) so both heads behave identically without duplicated code.
   - Re-measure the fit constants for the skin head (its silhouette gains hair volume), or make them per-head.
6. **Assets:**
   - `src/assets/skin-head.glb` (meshopt + quantization like today) and `src/assets/skin-head.jpg`, both generated by the script and reproducible byte-for-byte from the capture.
   - Never commit the raw capture or its original texture.
   - The copper outputs (`head.glb`, `head-face.jpg`) stay as they are.

## Constraints
- **Behaviour matches the copper head:** nod, sway, mouse follow, phone tilt, camera fit (desktop and phones), and reduced motion.
- **Performance:** per-frame cost unchanged. The glb plus atlas load comparably, with ≤ ~1.2 MB extra total.
- **Privacy:** the committed atlas contains only his face, hair and skin, and synthesized regions. No room, window or shirt pixels.
- **Ownership:**
