# Daylight: The Site Lit by the Visitor's Clock

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans (superpowers:test-driven-development for the pure daylight maths). Project skills that apply: `3d-model`, `tune-animation`, `create-animation`.

**Goal:** The page's lighting and colour follow the visitor's local time of day, so the same person sees a different site at 9am and 11pm:
- cold blue-white at dawn;
- neutral at midday;
- hot orange-copper at golden hour;
- deep violet with a cool rim light at night.

The head's reflections and highlights physically move as the "sun" arcs. It is purely visual, with no audio interaction.

**Owner's words** (2026-09-30): "3 unique crazy wacky ideas… purely visual, like motion design, colours, etc… no audio interactivity". Idea 1 was approved: "time-of-day sky, in reflections and colour".

## Design
1. **`src/choreography/daylight.ts`** is pure and tested. `daylight(date: Date, override?: number): Daylight` maps local time (fractional hour 0–24) to a continuous state:
   - `sun`: `{ azimuth, elevation }`, a simple arc: rises ~06:30, peaks ~13:00, sets ~19:30, below the horizon at night;
   - `key`: `{ color, intensity, position: [x, y, z] }`, the main light that orbits with the sun;
   - `rim`: `{ color, intensity }` (cool blue-violet at night);
   - `ambient`: `{ color, intensity }`;
   - `env`: the colours and intensities for the head's Lightformers (the top softbox takes the sky colour, the side panels warm or cool);
   - `palette`: page tokens:
     - `ground`, the page background, a small hue/lightness shift around `rgb(20, 61, 50)` that stays recognisably the site's green;
     - `gridBig` and `gridMini`, the plus colours;
     - `glassTint`, a subtle tint on the glass body/rim;
     - `accent`.

   Keyframes: pre-dawn 05:00, dawn 06:30, morning 09:00, midday 13:00, golden 18:30, dusk 20:00, night 22:30, deep night 02:00. Interpolate between them smoothly in a perceptual space (OKLab or linear RGB), with no visible steps; it wraps across midnight.
2. **`src/hooks/useDaylight.ts`:**
   - It re-evaluates about once a minute, not per frame.
   - It supports `?hour=18.5` to preview any time.
   - It supports `?daycycle` to sweep 24 h in 60 s for demos and review.
   - It exposes the current `Daylight`, and also writes the palette tokens to CSS custom properties on `:root` (`--day-ground`, `--day-grid-big`, …), so DOM layers can consume them without React.
3. **`<DaylightRig />`** (in `src/components/Head/DaylightRig.tsx` or `src/components/Daylight/`) is an r3f component that renders the Environment/Lightformers + key/rim/ambient lights from `useDaylight()`. It replaces the Head's hard-coded `<Environment>` block and the scene lights in `App.tsx`. It must be reusable by other heads: a `SkinHead` component is being built in parallel and will render `<DaylightRig variant="skin" />` at integration. Make the variant prop exist, with `skin` using gentler intensities.
   - **Midday must match today's look:** with `?hour=13` the scene is visually indistinguishable from the current site (same Lightformers and intensities, pinned by test). Other hours move away from it.
   - Performance: the env map is rebuilt at most when the daylight state changes meaningfully, about once a minute, never per frame. drei's `<Environment frames={1}>`, or a rebuild on change, is fine.
4. **Consumers of the palette:**
   - `Background` (grid canvas colours + ground);
   - `GlassPanel` (a tint on the glass's highlight/body layers, not on the refraction filter);
   - the `body`/`html` background.
   - Text stays white.
   - The Player skins and link dock are unchanged.
5. **Reduced motion:** the daylight still applies (it's colour, not motion), but `?daycycle` is disabled.
